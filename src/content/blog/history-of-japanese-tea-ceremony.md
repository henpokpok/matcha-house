The Japanese tea ceremony, known as *Chanoyu* or *Chado* ("The Way of Tea"), is a Choreographed ritual centered on the preparation and consumption of matcha green tea. 

Tracing its origins back to Zen Buddhist monks in the 9th century, the ceremony was refined in the 16th century by master Sen no Rikyu. At its core, the ceremony is built on four fundamental Zen principles:
*   **Wa (Harmony)**: Harmony between the guests, the host, the utensils, and the natural environment.
*   **Kei (Respect)**: Respect arising from the equality of all individuals in the tea room.
*   **Sei (Purity)**: Physical and spiritual cleanliness, represented by washing hands and sifting tea.
*   **Jaku (Tranquility)**: A serene state of mind achieved through the practice of the first three principles.
