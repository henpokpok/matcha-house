While matcha and loose-leaf green tea both come from the same plant (*Camellia sinensis*), they are vastly different in their growth, preparation, and nutritional content. 

In this article, we outline the three key differences between them:
1. **Shading Process**: Matcha leaves are shade-grown for weeks, which dramatically increases chlorophyll and amino acid levels. Standard green tea leaves remain exposed to sunlight.
2. **Consumption Method**: When you drink loose-leaf green tea, you steep the leaves and discard them. With matcha, you consume the entire tea leaf stone-ground into powder, delivering 10x the antioxidants.
3. **Texture and Taste**: Green tea is light, clear, and herbaceous. Matcha is thick, creamy, frothy, and packed with savory umami flavor.
