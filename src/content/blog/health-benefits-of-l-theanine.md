L-Theanine is a unique amino acid found almost exclusively in high-quality shade-grown green tea, particularly ceremonial grade matcha.

Unlike coffee, which can lead to jitters and an energy crash, the combination of caffeine and L-Theanine in matcha provides a sustained, calm focus. L-Theanine cross the blood-brain barrier to increase alpha wave activity, promoting relaxation without drowsiness. This synergy creates a state of "alert calm" that makes matcha the perfect companion for meditation, creative work, or mindful morning rituals.
