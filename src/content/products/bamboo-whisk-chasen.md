An indispensable tool for the traditional preparation of matcha. Our Artisan Bamboo Chasen (whisk) is hand-crafted by heritage artisans in Nara, Japan. Carved from a single piece of cured winter bamboo, it features 100 fine tines designed to suspend the powder evenly in water.

By whisking in a gentle, rapid 'W' or 'M' shape, this Chasen whips air into the matcha to create a smooth, creamy crema layer that softens the flavor profile and enhances the natural sweetness of your tea.
