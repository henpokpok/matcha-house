Sourced directly from the oldest tea estates in Uji, Japan, our Zenith Ceremonial Matcha is the epitome of purity. Shade-grown for 30 days before harvest, only the youngest, most tender leaves are hand-picked and stone-ground to preserve their vibrant green color and delicate umami flavor.

Experience a profoundly smooth, creamy texture with zero astringency. Ideal for traditional preparation (Koicha or Usucha) and daily mindfulness rituals. 100% organic and uncompromisingly pure.
