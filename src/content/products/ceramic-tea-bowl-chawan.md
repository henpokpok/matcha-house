Experience the tactile beauty of traditional Japanese ceramics. Our Wabi-Sabi Chawan (tea bowl) is handcrafted in Kyoto, featuring a slightly irregular, organic form that rests comfortably in both hands.

Finished with a textured, matte-charcoal and earthy metallic glaze, it is designed with a wide, flat bottom that allows the bamboo whisk to move freely, producing a superior, uniform froth. A timeless piece that elevates your tea preparation into a meditative practice.
